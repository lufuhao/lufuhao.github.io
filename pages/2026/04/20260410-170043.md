## IWGSC news

> It is with deep sadness that we share the news of the passing of Professor Philippa Borrill (John Innes Centre, UK). The wheat community has lost a brilliant scientist, a great collaborator and, above all, a wonderful human being. She will be greatly missed.
>
> Philippa worked on wheat since the beginning of her career. She joined the IWGSC in 2012 and became a member of the Coordinating Committee in 2018.
During her BBSRC Fellowship at the John Innes Centre in 2016, she worked alongside Ricardo Ramírez-González and Cristobal Uauy to lead the transcriptome analysis for the IWGSC RefSeq v1.0 project, assembling nearly all publicly available RNA-seq data for wheat into a landmark gene expression atlas published concurrently with the RefSeq v1.0 paper in Science in 2018.
> This resource [https://www.wheat-expression.com/] has since been used by thousands of researchers worldwide and has played a critical role in advancing wheat research and plant breeding. In recognition of this extraordinary contribution, Philippa, together with Ricardo and Cristobal, were awarded the IWGSC Leadership Award in 2018.
>
> We will remember Philippa for her brilliant mind, her intellectual curiosity, and her genuine commitment to making science more open, accessible, and collaborative. Her passing is a tremendous loss, not only for the wheat community, but for science as a whole.
>
> The IWGSC extends its deepest condolences to Philippa's family, her colleagues at the John Innes Centre, and all who knew her.
